\section{Limitations and Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\paragraph{Limitations.}
\R targets single-clock digital designs on open process kits; multi-clock, clock-domain-crossing and
test insertion are out of scope. The repair recipes were learned on the same development corpus they
are evaluated on; generalisation to held-out designs is \PH{held-out result or future work}. A
deterministic repair pipeline makes verdicts insensitive to the orchestrating model by construction,
so model comparisons inside \R measure cost, not repair ability. \PH{other limitations}
