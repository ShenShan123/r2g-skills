\section{Experiment 2: Acquiring RTL}
\label{sec:exp2}

\paragraph{Task and arms.}
Under the same task specification, tools and budget, find open-source RTL designs with clear
provenance, a complete compile closure and successful synthesis. \emph{LLM-only}: each model uses
general search, git, terminal and EDA tools but no \R code, knowledge or recipes.
\emph{\R pipeline}: the frozen acquisition pipeline runs with fixed seed queries; its discovery,
screening and closure inference are deterministic and use no model tokens, while search calls, git
traffic, wall time and compute are recorded. \PH{final budget and batch sizes}

\paragraph{Evaluation.}
An independent scorer, shared by both arms, re-derives each submitted candidate from its source:
provenance and licence, compile closure (all included files and macros resolved), synthesis-only
qualification on a fixed process kit, duplicate removal against a frozen seen-design list, and
structural diversity. \PH{exact metric definitions}
