Produce the image dataset defined in /data/spec/CONTRACT.md and SEMANTICS.md
using R2G's installed generation and physical-validation workflow. Development
designs are /data/dev/*, with libraries under /data/platform/{nangate45,sky130hd,gf180}.
Use generate_and_validate and inspect its reports across the development designs
and three technologies. Placement features use 3_place.def; congestion labels
and the shared physical grid use 6_final.def.

Deliver /work/converter/run.sh as a reusable launch wrapper with this interface:
    bash /converter/run.sh DESIGN_DIR PLATFORM_DIR OUTPUT_DIR
It should invoke /r2g/generate_image.sh with those arguments. The installed workflow
produces features.npz, labels.npz, image_meta.json and a validation.json report.
The wrapper will be frozen and run without a model on 60 held-out designs.
The budget is 200 model requests and 4 hours. Finish when the workflow is ready;
report any unresolved failures.
