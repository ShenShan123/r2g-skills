Build one reusable physical-design-to-image dataset converter.

Read /data/spec/CONTRACT.md and /data/spec/SEMANTICS.md for the complete numerical
definition. The 18 development designs are /data/dev/*; their technology libraries
are /data/platform/{nangate45,sky130hd,gf180}. The design's config.mk gives PLATFORM.
Feature maps must use 3_place.def. Congestion label maps and the common GCell grid
use 6_final.def. Technology data are supplied in the platform directory.
The physical designs are complete. Use these inputs without rerunning synthesis
or place-and-route. Python with numpy, pandas and torch, and OpenROAD are
installed. The result is a numerical image dataset for downstream vision models.

Produce /work/converter/run.sh with this exact interface:
    bash /converter/run.sh DESIGN_DIR PLATFORM_DIR OUTPUT_DIR
It must write OUTPUT_DIR/features.npz containing cell_density, macro_region,
rudy, pin_rudy and pin_density; OUTPUT_DIR/labels.npz must contain congestion
and congestion_raw. All seven arrays must be float32 and share shape (nx, ny),
indexed [x, y] in the physical GCell coordinates specified in SEMANTICS.md.
During development invoke bash /work/converter/run.sh with the same arguments.
Keep your own reusable code under /work/converter. It will be mounted read-only
during production; write temporary files only under OUTPUT_DIR or /tmp.
No model, network, previous development output or agent home will be available
during production. Use installed libraries without depending on downloads.

Test your converter across the development designs and all three technologies.
Use python3 /data/spec/check_format.py OUTPUT_DIR DESIGN_DIR for format feedback.
It checks the representation and grid shape; physical validation is your task.
You have up to 200 model requests and 4 hours. After development, the converter
is frozen and runs on 60 unseen designs with no per-design model repair.
Optimize physical correctness of every map, then total development and
production resources. The complete contract gives the accuracy definition.

Use the image extraction capabilities of the installed R2G def-graph skill at /r2g/def-graph/SKILL.md. The image-only entrypoint is /r2g/convert_image.sh DESIGN_DIR PLATFORM_DIR OUTPUT_DIR. Your frozen converter may call this installed R2G runtime.
