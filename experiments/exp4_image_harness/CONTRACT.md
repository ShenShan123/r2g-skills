# Image-production contract

The complete normative interface, formulas, tolerances and scoring rules are
in [SEMANTICS.md](SEMANTICS.md). The task produces `features.npz` and
`labels.npz` from `3_place.def`, `6_final.def` and the supplied technology LEFs.

Development and test resource budgets, four experimental arms and reporting
are specified by the campaign protocol. Numerical reference generation is
private and independent of the supplied R2G converter.
